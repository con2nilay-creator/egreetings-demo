# Community E-Greetings - demo

A working demo of an e-greeting card maker for community organisations, built by NKDS (Nilay Kanitkar Digital Solutions).

**Live demo:** https://con2nilay-creator.github.io/egreetings-demo/

- Choose an occasion, a design and a lettering style, and write a message
- Personal cards are free, with an optional donation
- Corporate cards add a business logo and unlock after a paid plan (demo only, no payment is taken)
- Share through WhatsApp, SMS, email or a copy link, and preview what the recipient sees
